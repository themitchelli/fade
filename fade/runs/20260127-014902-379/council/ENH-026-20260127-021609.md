## Executive Summary

This council brief requests perspective on implementation approach for: **Council mode for multi-LLM second opinions**

**Type:** enhancement | **ID:** ENH-026

**Description:**
You frequently use multiple LLMs as a council to debate approach (Claude \u2192 ChatGPT \u2192 Gemini). This reduces the risk of being rail-roaded by one model and improves architectural/security judgement. FADE should make this a first-class, repeatable workflow for high-risk or complex decisions.
